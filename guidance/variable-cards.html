wspeed — 10 m wind speed (scalar)

Definition. Scalar daily-mean 10 m wind speed: the hourly wind speeds are averaged to daily. It is not the magnitude of the daily-mean vector components.

Datasets LOCA2 CA hybrid (3 km); WRF (WUS-D3) also carries native wind
Units m/s
Temporal daily
Type separately downscaled, independently validated scalar field
Provenance GCM -> LOCA2 hybrid downscaling (WRF pattern library) -> wspeed
Use wspeed for wind-speed magnitude, directly
Don’t confuse with uas, vas (vector components) - their magnitude is not equal to wspeed

Known issue — DQ4 (resolved). wspeed and sqrt(uas^2 + vas^2) differ at daily resolution by design. Wind speed (positive-definite) and the vector components (signed) are downscaled and bias-corrected separately, with different extreme-value handling, so the two are not equal and wspeed must not be reconstructed from the daily components. The difference is centred positive (scalar pedestal) with a left-skewed tail from the differing bias-correction of the two distributions. All three fields agree well seasonally. Guidance (Scripps): use the wspeed variable directly. Confirmed R20.

Validated: Scripps CA-hybrid seasonal validation (R04), RMSE ~0.4-0.5%; vs ERA5 1980-2010 head-to-head (Q05, v2): both downscaled products high, timing agrees; LOCA2 +29% is closer than dynamical WRF-GCM +69%; offset worst over flat interior. The earlier PresRat explanation is rejected - origin is upstream of downscaling. Single member; WRF carries a sampling-frequency caveat
Evidence: R04, R15, Q03, R20, Q05
Source: D. Pierce (Scripps), personal communication, August 2026 [confirm exact date] — see R20.
Status: Confirmed (Scripps, R20)

uas — eastward 10 m wind component

Definition. Eastward (zonal) component of the 10 m wind, as a daily-mean vector component. Signed: positive = eastward. Averaged as a vector, so opposing sub-daily flow cancels.

Datasets LOCA2 CA hybrid (3 km); WRF (WUS-D3) native
Units m/s
Temporal daily
Type vector component (signed)
Provenance GCM -> LOCA2 hybrid downscaling (WRF pattern library) -> uas
Use uas for wind direction and vector operations (paired with vas)
Don’t confuse with wspeed - a scalar speed; sqrt(uas2+vas2) does not equal wspeed (see the wspeed card)

Known issue — DQ4 (resolved). Do not build wind speed from the daily components - use wspeed. See the wspeed card and R20 for the confirmed explanation.

Validated: Scripps CA-hybrid seasonal validation (R04), RMSE ~0.0 m/s
Evidence: R04, R15, Q03
Status: Confirmed (Scripps, R20)
